Most people have never heard of it.

Fewer still have played it.

TienGow is a four-player domino game faster and less forgiving than mahjong, a Chinese national essence that has survived centuries yet remains largely lost outside the communities that kept it alive.

SkyNine Social Club gathers across New York City to play it, teach it, and bring it forward.
