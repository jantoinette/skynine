The Tiles

There are 2 suits in the game of TienGow: Scholar and Military. They are ranked highest to lowest from left to right.

The design of these tiles are based on different dice combinations.

Legal Hands

To beat another tile, the tile must be of the same suit, and in not an equal but a higher ranking.

Single Tiles

Each tile can be played individually.

Matching Pairs

The exact same tile can be played together as a pair.

Mixed Sets

Any combinations of Scholar and its Military counterpart: Heaven/Nine, Earth/Eight, Man/Seven, Harmony/Five.

Supreme

One of a kind. Unbeatable if led, but a discard if not.

The Rules

The Goal

Play the strongest hand and outscore your opponents in the final round.

The Set

  1. Highest dice roll deals first game.
  2. Shuffle the tiles. Arrange them into 8 stacks of 4.
  3. Before the dice fall, player opposite to dealer decides the pattern of how each player collects their 8 tiles,
  4. Count the rolled number counter-clockwise. That player draws first.

    5. The Play

  5. Dealer leads the first round and sets the hand. Play moves counter-clockwise.
  6. Beat what's on the table. If you can't — or choose not to — discard the same number of tiles face-down. Sacrifice what you deem unworthy. No one can see what you've given up.
  7. Highest hand of the round wins the stacks, and leads the next.
  8. In a single-tile final round, those without any stacks are disqualified and must discard their hand. No exceptions.
  9. Winner of final round rises to dealer's seat. Table resets.

Scoring

  • Par is 4 stacks. Win more and you score the difference. Win fewer and you lose it. Win nothing, and you lose five points automatically.
  • Dealer plays for double — winnings and losses. Stay winner across consecutive games and the multiplier climbs by one each time. The longer you hold the table, the more you stand to gain. And lose.
  • Playing Supreme wins you 2 points from all players. Any Quartet, 4 points. Anyone who plays Supreme gets 2 instant points, Quartet 4 instant points. Paid immediately by every other player at the table.
  • If a Quartet, Supreme, or Three Chickens wins the final round, points are doubled. Immediate wins and dealer's multiplier are honored.
  • Sweeping all 8 stacks doubles the game. Multipliers compound.
